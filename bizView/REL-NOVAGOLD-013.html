Signal
On an earnings call, management states an intention to raise, refinance, repay, or seek advice on financing — including appointing advisors to evaluate funding options.
Why it matters
This is the most direct financing signal available anywhere in this catalogue: the client's own CFO or CEO describing what they intend to do about funding, on the record, usually quarters before it appears in a filing. Direction matters and both directions are useful — stated intent to *raise* or to *evaluate funding alternatives* is an immediate origination lead, often with a live mandate process already running; stated intent to *deleverage* or *prepay* signals reduced near-term borrowing appetite but an improving credit profile and a possible refinancing/repricing conversation, and is a retention flag if the debt being repaid is the bank's own (cross-check EAF22).
