Why it matters
A high disclosed rate versus peers, OR a materially RISEN own-rate, is a pricing-benchmark input when structuring a competing refinancing offer — the client is paying more than it recently was, or more than peers currently do. A materially FALLEN own-rate is a different, equally real opportunity: the client has already benefited from favorable conditions and is a candidate for locking in a long-term facility at today's rate before conditions reverse, or for a rate-hedging product that protects the gain. An undisclosed or unhedged floating exposure is a direct rate-hedging cross-sell, independent of either rate-level signal.
