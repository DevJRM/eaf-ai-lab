Why it matters
Untied facilities are underwritten against the home country's strategic interest, not against a specific export contract — so the question they turn on is "does this counterparty sit in a supply chain our country needs secured?", which is a structural fact about the client rather than an event in its calendar. Case (a) is the classic untied target: financing a foreign producer in exchange for supply security, which is what Cesce's Póliza de Inversiones Estratégicas and SACE's Push-type facilities are built to do. Case (b) identifies the domestic industrial whose dependency creates the strategic interest in the first place — useful both as a lead in its own right and as the justification an untied application needs.
