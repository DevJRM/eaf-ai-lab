EAF28 · Free cash flow deficit against disclosed capex opened
Operating cash flow minus capex is negative, or has deteriorated materially, while capex itself is flat or rising.
A client spending more on capex than it generates from operations cannot fund its investment program from its own cash flow alone — it must draw down cash reserves, raise debt, or raise equity. This is the cleanest, most standard calculation available to confirm a financing need is real rather than optional, and the free-cash-flow deficit itself is a rough proxy for the size of the gap.
